Minimum hourly pay top-ups

DFRNT works out each driver's hours from their jobs, compares what their jobs earned with their guaranteed hourly rate over the pay period, and suggests a top-up for a manager to approve. Dispatchers can see who is falling behind, so they can steer work their way, without ever seeing anyone's pay rate.

This is a plan. The screens below are mock-ups with made-up drivers, not live screens.

What it does

Hours

Hours from the jobs

Each driver's day runs from their first pickup to their last drop. No timesheets to fill in.

Hours

Minimum per shift

A short day is paid at the driver's minimum, for example 3 or 4 hours.

Pay

Across the pay period

Hours and earnings add up over the fortnight, so a good day can make up for a quiet one.

Pay

Approve, then pay

A manager approves each suggested top-up. It goes on the driver's next pay run as an extra payment.

Dispatch

See who's behind

Drivers who are behind are highlighted on the dispatch board each morning, with how far behind they are.

Privacy

Pay rates stay private

Hourly rates and minimum hours are set in Accounts. Operations staff never see them.

  • Guaranteed: 30.5 paid hours × $40.00 = $1,220.00
  • Jobs earned: $1,049.00
  • Suggested top-up: $1,220.00 − $1,049.00 = $171.00. If the jobs earn more than the guarantee, there's no top-up.
  • A manager can correct a day's hours, for example when a driver finished early for an appointment, broke down, or sat on their last job. Every change needs a reason.

What stays the same

  • Drivers are paid per job exactly as they are now. The top-up is an extra payment on top.
  • Drivers not set as eligible are unaffected.
  • Pay runs, RCTIs and deductions work as they do today.
  • Dispatchers' screens only gain the highlight and the amount behind.
